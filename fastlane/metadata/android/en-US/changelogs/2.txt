Each widget can have its own theme (System, Light, Dark or Black); black widgets get an outline. Widgets show O and P prices and bigger charts. Charts fill their width with no dashed line. Pick as many indices as you like. Runs at your screen's highest refresh rate, such as 120 Hz. Key stats are now tiles with more data: day change, day range, distance from the 52-week high and low, value traded, type, trading hours and last trade.
