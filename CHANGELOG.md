# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Each widget can have its own theme (System, Light, Dark or Black), chosen when you add it or reconfigure it. Pure black widgets now have a faint outline so they stay visible on a black wallpaper.
- Widgets show the open and previous close as "O" and "P" so the prices fit, and their charts are larger.
- Charts in widgets and on the home list fill their whole width and no longer draw the dashed previous-close line.
- Pick as many indices as you like for the home strip.
- The app runs at the screen's highest refresh rate (e.g. 120 Hz).
- Key stats are shown as tiles and add day change, day range, distance from the 52-week high and low, value traded, currency, type, trading hours and last trade time.

## 1.0.0

The first release of the rebuilt app.

- New home: indices strip, portfolio summary, market status, sorting, and an edit mode to reorder, pin and remove stocks.
- Stock detail: charts for 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, 5Y and Max with drag-to-inspect, key stats, pre- and after-hours prices, news, notes and sharing.
- Holdings with average cost, and a portfolio total in your home currency.
- Price alerts: above or below a price, or a daily move.
- Six widgets: stock list, one stock, two stocks, ticker strip, portfolio and chart. They follow the system theme and can be reconfigured.
- Light, Dark and System themes, pure black, and wallpaper colors on Android 12+.
- Tablet and foldable two-pane layout, app-icon shortcuts and haptic feedback.
- One-day charts follow the trading day: the line fills in across the session, and before the open they show the last session instead of nothing.
- Smarter background refresh that pauses while markets are closed.
- Fixes from the previous version: prices are no longer cleared when a refresh fails, London prices in pence are shown correctly, very small prices keep their digits, numbers follow your locale, and today's change is measured from the previous close.
