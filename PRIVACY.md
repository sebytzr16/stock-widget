# Privacy

Stock Widget has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your device

Your list, holdings, price alerts, notes, widget choices and settings are saved in the app's private storage. The last downloaded prices are cached there too, so the app and widgets open instantly.

If Android backup is turned on, your list, holdings, alerts, notes and settings are included in it; cached prices are not.

## What goes over the network

The app connects only to Yahoo Finance (`query1.finance.yahoo.com` and `query2.finance.yahoo.com`) to fetch:

- prices and charts for the symbols in your list, your widgets and the indices strip,
- exchange rates needed to total your portfolio in your home currency,
- search results for what you type in search,
- news headlines for the stock you're viewing.

Like any website, Yahoo sees your IP address and the symbols requested. Nothing else is sent: not your holdings, notes or alerts. Opening a news headline opens it in your browser.

## Permissions

- **Internet** and **network state**: to fetch prices, and to wait for a connection before refreshing.
- **Notifications** (Android 13+): only for price alerts, and only requested when you create your first alert.
